ELENA'S CAFÉ – SOCIAL MEDIA PROMOTION PLAN

Client: Elena's Café

What product/service is being promoted?

Elena's Café offers a variety of coffee drinks, including espresso, americano, latte, cappuccino, iced caramel macchiato, and matcha drinks. The café also offers desserts and baked treats such as cheesecake and cookies.

Who is the target audience?

The target audience is coffee lovers, students, young adults, professionals, and people who enjoy relaxing at a café while having coffee and desserts. The café also targets customers who enjoy sharing food and drink experiences on social media.

Which platforms are most suitable?

Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok are suitable platforms for Elena's Café. Instagram can be used for attractive photos of coffee and desserts, Facebook can be used for promotions and updates, and TikTok can be used for short videos showing drinks, desserts, and café experiences.

Proposed Hashtags:

#ElenaCafe
#CoffeeLovers
#CoffeeTime
#CafeLife
#FreshCoffee

Promotional Strategy:

Elena's Café will use Instagram and TikTok to share attractive photos and short videos of its coffee drinks and desserts. Facebook will be used to share promotions, café updates, and new menu items. The strategy will focus on visually appealing content, relevant hashtags, and engaging posts to attract coffee lovers and encourage customers to visit the café.
